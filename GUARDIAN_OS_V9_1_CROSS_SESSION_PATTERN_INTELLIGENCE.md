# Guardian OS V9.1 — Cross-Session Pattern Intelligence

V9.1 turns local Decision Memory into deterministic longitudinal evidence.

It identifies recurring risk-escalation timing, recovery behaviour, personal EAR
trends, recurring perception limitations, time-of-day risk associations,
escalation/recovery recurrence and the historical sessions most similar to the
current state.

Each pattern carries a confidence value, supporting-session count, evidence,
trend label and an explicit trust limitation. Similarity is transparent and
uses summary-distance comparisons over advisory risk, EAR, time period and
perception state.

V9.1 also includes the V9.0.1 UX consistency fix: when Monitoring is OFF,
Guardian now recommends starting live monitoring rather than incorrectly saying
that live monitoring remains active.

Safety boundaries are unchanged. Cross-session intelligence does not retrain
the model, alter calibration, change alert thresholds, weaken Passport or
Perception trust gates, or infer medical causation from correlations.
